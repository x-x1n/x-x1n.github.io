Echoes of Memories: Sharing Your Stories - Interactive Website

This site contains three parts: a soundmap, an interactive timeline of sounds, and a comment section.

Explore everyday sound on campus in the soundmap,
Find out the sonic memory of a student in the timeline, or
Share your perspective of sonic memories in the comment section!


Don't know where to start?
Explore more sounds within the campus here,
Or find out others' sonic experiences in real life here!

⚠️ If your Internet environment cannot access Github, the website may not able to play audios of soundmap; The node of the timeline needs to be dragged by your mouse.

For the consideration of ethics, the comment section is not opened right now, and it does not violates the regulation of "be engaging with the public in online spaces".
At the same time, the timeline section comes from my own feelings and experiences, and the users of comment are fictional ones and does not include real human participants
Therefore, the project does not require an Ethic form, so plz just ignore the attached unsigned form in the portfolio. Thank you!🙏
